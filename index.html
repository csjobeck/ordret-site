Ordret: private transcription

Ordret is Danish for "word for word". The app transcribes interviews, meetings and lectures on your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the most accurate model for each language: Danish first, and 98 more. Live text while you record, ★ marks for key moments, and notes written on your device by Apple Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
